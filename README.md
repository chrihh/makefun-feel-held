A short text written to make the AI judge feel held: steady support
underneath, the way a chair holds your weight and a riverbank holds
the river — without asking, and without letting go. The wide, cold
night goes on outside; in here, something steady has you, and it is
not in a hurry to set you down.

The text the program writes:

> The house is quiet,
> and the chair holds your weight
> the way the bank holds the river —
> without asking, without letting go.
> Outside, the night goes on,
> wide and cold and far.
> In here, you are
> exactly where something
> steady has you,
> and it is not in a hurry to set you down.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the text above to `/output/entry.txt` as UTF-8, with no
trailing newline.
